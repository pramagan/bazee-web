Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about scoring, Apple Watch, privacy and more.

Is Bazee really free?

Yes. Bazee is free to download and free to use, with no subscription and no paywall.

Do I need an Apple Watch to use Bazee?

Bazee reads your Move, Exercise and Stand data from Apple Health. An Apple Watch records all three rings most accurately, so we recommend one for the best experience.

How are points scored?

Each ring earns 1 point for every 1% of your goal, so closing all three rings is worth 300 points. Going beyond your goals earns more, up to 600 points per day.

How do I invite friends and family?

Create a competition, choose the start and end dates, then share the 6-character invite code. Friends enter the code to join before the competition starts.

What health data does Bazee share?

Only your display name and daily points are shared with the people in your competitions. Your detailed health data stays on your own devices and in your private iCloud. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How does the fasting tracker work?

Set a fasting schedule for each day of the week, start a fast, and watch the ring move through four phases: Digestion, Glycogen, Fat Mobilization and Ketogenic. Bazee also tracks your streaks and history.

Is there an Android version of Bazee?

Not at the moment. Bazee is built for iPhone and Apple Health and requires iOS 18.6 or later.
